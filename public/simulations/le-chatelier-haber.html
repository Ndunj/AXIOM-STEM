Equilibrium Principles & Le Châtelier's Law

Le Châtelier's principle states that if a dynamic equilibrium system is subjected to a change in temperature, pressure, or species concentrations, the system shifts its position to partially counteract the disturbance.

Reaction Quotient ($Q_c$) vs Equilibrium Constant ($K_c$)

For the gas-phase Haber process, the concentration reaction quotient is calculated as:

Q_c = [NH₃]² / ([N₂] · [H₂]³) = (n_NH₃² · V²) / (n_N₂ · n_H₂³)
  • Q_c < K_c: The forward reaction is favored to convert reactants into products (shift right).
  • Q_c > K_c: The reverse reaction is favored to consume excess product (shift left).
  • Q_c = K_c: The system is at dynamic equilibrium (no net change in concentrations).
