📚 Mitosis vs. Meiosis: High School Study Guide

1. Purpose & Cell Types

• Mitosis: Used for growth, tissue repair, and asexual reproduction in somatic (body) cells. Produces 2 genetically identical diploid (2n) daughter cells.
• Meiosis: Used to form gametes (sperm and egg cells) for sexual reproduction. Involves two rounds of division to produce 4 genetically unique haploid (n) daughter cells.

2. Diagram Structure Reference Key

  • Cell Membrane: Outer boundary that pinches (cleavage furrow) during cytokinesis.
  • Nuclear Envelope: Dashed yellow boundary enclosing chromatin during interphase; dissolves in prophase.
  • Centrosome & Spindle Fibers: Yellow structures at poles generating microtubule fibers that move chromosomes.
  • Chromosomes: Color-coded blue (paternal origin) and red (maternal origin) with white central centromeres.
