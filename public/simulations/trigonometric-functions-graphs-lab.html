Trigonometric Transformations & Formula Guide

General Transformation Form

y = A func(B(x - C)) + D

Every parameter modifies the shape and position of the standard trigonometric wave on the Cartesian plane.

1. Amplitude (A) Controls the vertical stretch or compression. If A is negative, the graph reflects across its midline. Max/Min values are given by D + |A| and D - |A|.
2. Frequency / Angular Velocity (B) Determines the wave frequency. The period T of sine and cosine waves is calculated as T = 2 * pi / |B|.
3. Phase Shift / Horizontal Shift (C) Shifts the graph horizontally to the right by C units (if x - C) or to the left (if x + C).
4. Vertical Shift (D) Shifts the entire sinusoidal midline up or down by D units (equation of midline: y = D).

Asymptotes & Reciprocal Functions

Functions like tan(x), csc(x), sec(x), and cot(x) feature vertical asymptotes where the denominator equals zero, resulting in undefined vertical bounds.
