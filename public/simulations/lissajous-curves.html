• X(t) = A·sin(a·t + δ) represents horizontal motion driven by a tuned harmonic oscillator.

• Y(t) = B·sin(b·t) represents vertical motion driven by a perpendicular oscillator.

• When the ratio a/b is rational, the trajectory forms a closed loop. The number of horizontal tangency points equals b, and vertical tangencies equal a.
