Electrochemical Principles & The Nernst Equation

A galvanic cell converts chemical energy into electrical potential using a spontaneous redox reaction. Electrons flow through the external circuit from the Anode (oxidation, lower E°) to the Cathode (reduction, higher E°).

Does Standard Cell Potential (E°cell) Depend on Concentration?

No, E°cell does NOT depend on electrolyte concentrations. By definition, standard potential is measured strictly under standard conditions where all solute concentrations are fixed at 1.0 M, gas pressures are 1 atm, and temperature is 25°C (298.15 K). Because standard conditions explicitly fix concentrations at 1.0 M, E°cell remains a constant baseline value.

Standard Potential vs. Non-Standard Cell Potential

  • Standard Cell Potential (E°cell): A fixed constant for a specific pair of metals at a given temperature. It does not change when concentration changes.
  • Non-Standard Cell Potential (Ecell): The actual operating voltage of the battery. It dynamically changes as electrolyte concentrations change, governed by the Nernst Equation.

The Nernst Equation Formulation

Ecell = E°cell - (R · T / n · F) · ln(Q)
