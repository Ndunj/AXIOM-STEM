📘 System & Simulation Notes

Welcome to SatCom-Sim 3D, an interactive physical visualizer and RF link budget simulator featuring a 4-satellite constellation with interactive voice explanations.

Constellation Architecture:

  • Identical Satellite Geometry: Each satellite utilizes identical high-detail spacecraft geometry featuring MLI foil buses, dual solar wings, parabolic dishes, phased array antennas, and thrusters.
  • Color Differentiation: Satellites feature distinct color schemes on both their MLI bus foil and solar panel array wings, corresponding to their orbital beam vectors (Cyan, Magenta, Emerald, and Amber).
  • Voice Synthesis Explanations: Built-in real-time speech narration automatically explains the dynamics, latency, and operational features of every selected orbital regime.

RF Physics Engine Details:

  • FSPL (Free Space Path Loss): Signal loss dynamically calculated over slant range and carrier frequency.
  • Link Budget Tracking: Use the dropdown in the Telemetry Panel to monitor telemetry metrics for any individual satellite in real time.
