📘 System & Simulation Notes

Welcome to SatCom-Sim 3D, an interactive physical visualizer and RF link budget simulator for satellite communications.

Key Visual Features:

  • Realistic Spacecraft Bus: Detailed gold-foil MLI insulation, high-gain parabolic dishes, phased-array panels, and blue silicon solar arrays.
  • Dynamic Orbital Motion: Orbit speeds are significantly accelerated (250x base default) to observe real-time handovers and elevation changes across LEO, MEO, GEO, and HEO orbits.

RF Physics Engine Details:

  • FSPL (Free Space Path Loss): Calculates signal degradation across distance and frequency using ITU-R formulas.
  • Rain Attenuation: Simulates atmospheric absorption based on millimeter rain rates and elevation angle look-vector.
  • Link Margin: Evaluates carrier-to-noise ratio against a 6.0 dB QPSK demodulation threshold. Active beam locks when above horizon.
