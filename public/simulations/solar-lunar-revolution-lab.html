Explanatory Notes & Concepts

1. Absolute Heliocentric Reference Frame The Sun remains anchored at the coordinate origin $(0,0,0)$. The Earth revolves around the Sun in a 365-day elliptical path while maintaining its continuous 24-hour axial rotation.
2. Orbital Inclination ($5.14^\circ$) The Moon's orbital plane is tilted at $5.14^\circ$ relative to the ecliptic plane (Earth's orbital plane around the Sun). Because of this tilt, lunar and solar eclipses do not occur every single month, but only when the Moon crosses the ecliptic nodes during full or new moon alignment.
3. Umbra Shadow & Blood Moon Dynamics The Umbra is the dark central cone of Earth's shadow. When the Moon passes completely inside this cone ($100\%$ penetration), Earth blocks direct sunlight. Rayleigh scattering through Earth's atmosphere filters blue light and refracts deep red wavelengths onto the lunar surface, causing the characteristic "Blood Moon" appearance.
