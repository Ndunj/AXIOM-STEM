Mathematical & Physical Principles

Unit Circle Coordinates: A point on the unit circle (radius R = 1) centered at (0,0) has coordinates (x, y) = (cosθ, sinθ). Quadrant I ranges from 0° to 90°, Quadrant II from 90° to 180°, Quadrant III from 180° to 270°, and Quadrant IV from 270° to 360°.

Simple Harmonic Motion & Waves: Manually advancing the angle θ maps the vertical sine height onto the continuous wave display on the right, directly illustrating wave amplitude oscillation.

Phasor Vectors in Electromagnetism: In AC circuit analysis and physics optics, time-varying signals are modeled as rotating phasors. The physical measurable quantity corresponds to the real-axis horizontal cosine projection E(t) = E0 cos(θ).
