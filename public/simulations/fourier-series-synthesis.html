Fourier Series and Harmonic Decomposition Lab

Harmonic Synthesis Suite & Epicycles
Fourier Series: Any periodic waveform can be decomposed into an infinite sum of sine and cosine harmonic waves at integer multiples of the fundamental frequency.

Epicycles: Rotating phasor circles visually represent individual harmonic terms. Their vector sum traces out the time-domain waveform.

Gibbs Phenomenon: When synthesizing discontinuous waveforms (square or sawtooth) with finite terms, a persistent ~9% overshoot occurs near discontinuities.
