The Geometric Logic

Both arrangements are enclosed within the exact same outer square of side length (a + b), giving a total outer area of (a + b)².

  • Four Triangles: Each arrangement contains four identical right-angled triangles with area ½ab. Total area of 4 triangles = 2ab.
  • State 1: The 4 triangles are paired into two rectangles, leaving two inner empty squares of area a² and b².
  • State 2: The same 4 triangles are moved to the four corners of the outer square, leaving a single centered tilted square with all four sides equal to c and area c².
  • Conclusion: Since both configurations subtract the exact same 4 triangles from the same overall square, the remaining inner area must be equal: a² + b² = c².
