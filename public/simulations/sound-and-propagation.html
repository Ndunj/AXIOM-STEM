Guided Discovery & Wave Mechanics

1. Pitch vs Frequency:
Pitch is the human brain's auditory perception of sound frequency. High frequency produces a high-pitched note with tightly packed wave compressions (short wavelength lambda). Low frequency creates a deep, low-pitched rumble with widely spaced compressions (long wavelength lambda).

2. Physical Pressure Waveform:
Sound waves in real life are longitudinal pressure variations ΔP. In the enlarged oscilloscope view, wave crests represent Compressions (high density / positive pressure ΔP) and troughs represent Rarefactions (low density / negative pressure ΔP).

3. Medium Elasticity & Wave Speed (v = f * lambda):
When sound travels through denser and more elastic media like Steel (5,100 m/s) compared to Air (343 m/s), the wave speed v increases drastically. Because frequency f remains tied to the acoustic source, the wavelength lambda stretches significantly in solids!
